# Hackathon instructions
Build an AI system that turns a specific new product idea that an enterprise innovation manager has come up with into something their stakeholders can see and touch. Users (innovation managers at Fortune 500s who are trying to launch new products and services) input their company and drop in supporting documents (eg. a corporate strategy, annual report, etc), the system then researches the brand, maps out the idea (for example, Caffeinated Chewing Gum by Red Bull), sketches the design, and generates an interactive 3D prototype that the innovation manager could iterate on and take to their product development teams to prototype. We're looking for end-to-end pipelines that take a user from a hypothetical product idea to a tangible product.
